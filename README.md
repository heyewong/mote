### 为什么选择 Mote？

大多数 AI 工具都活在另一个浏览器标签页里。你不得不从 Obsidian 复制一段笔记，粘贴进 AI 工具，费劲解释半天前因后果，再把结果拷回来手动排版。在手机上，这个过程更是令人崩溃。

**Mote 把 AI 真正带进了你的知识库。**

无论你是在电脑前梳理复杂项目，还是在路上捕捉灵感，Mote 都能在你的 Vault 旁边贴身协作。它基于你选定的笔记上下文思考、帮你建立关联、起草修改建议——而最终的确认键，始终握在你手里。

## Why Mote?

Most AI tools live in a separate browser tab. You copy a thought from Obsidian, paste it into an AI tool, explain the background, copy the answer back, and format it by hand. On mobile, that process gets even harder.

**Mote brings AI directly into your notes.** Whether you’re shaping a project at your desk or capturing an idea on the go, Mote works alongside your Vault. Bring in the notes that matter, connect ideas, and draft changes while staying in control of what gets written.

---

## Key Highlights

### 🔍 Grounded in Your Own Notes

Ask questions based on the notes you choose to bring in. Mote can search and read relevant files in your Vault, giving you an AI companion that works with the knowledge you have already collected.

### 🛡️ Review Every Proposed Change

Your notes are your intellectual property. When Mote drafts an update, rewrites a passage, or suggests a new note, you can review the proposal and decide whether to approve it.

### 📱 Desktop and Mobile

Mote is built for Obsidian on desktop and mobile. Open your Vault, pick up where you left off, and keep thinking wherever you are.

### ⚙️ Bring Your Own Key (BYOK)

Use your own AI provider account and API key. Choose the model service that works for you without buying a bundled Mote model plan.

## Quick Start

1. **Install Mote**: Download the latest package from the [Releases](../../releases) page or install through BRAT.
2. **Add Your Key**: Open Obsidian Settings → **Mote**, choose your AI provider, and enter your API key.
3. **Start Thinking**: Open the Mote sidebar or mobile shortcut and start working with your Vault.

Your 14-day full-featured trial begins when you first start the Mote Agent.

---

## Privacy & BYOK

- **No telemetry by default**: Mote does not collect telemetry by default and includes no ads.
- **Your chosen provider**: Prompts and the note context you bring into a request are sent to your configured AI provider to generate a response.
- **Secure key storage**: API keys are stored through Obsidian SecretStorage.
- **You approve Vault changes**: Review Mote’s proposed writes and decide whether to approve them.
